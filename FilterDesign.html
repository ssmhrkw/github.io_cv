Butterworth 1/n‑Octave Filter Designer (IEC 61260:1995 limits)

ツール一覧
Filter = HP(f₁) × LP(f₂) via pre‑warped bilinear transform; orders split ⌊M/2⌋ (HP) and ⌈M/2⌉ (LP). Cascade gain normalized at fm.
Plot is in ΔA space: ΔA(f) = −|H(f)| (dB) with |H(fm)| ≈ 0 dB. Limits are the Class 0/1/2 attenuation limits of IEC 61260:1995 (JIS C 1514:2002), drawn only where defined; +∞ regions are not drawn. Mid-band frequencies and band edges use the base-ten ratio G = 100.3. IEC 61260-1:2014 (Class 1/2 acceptance limits) is not implemented here.
